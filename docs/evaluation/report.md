# Evaluation report

## What was measured

- Datasets: **test** (1500 messages), **handwritten** (43 messages). All data is synthetic or hand-written; no real person's data is used.
- **Matching rule:** a detection is correct when it has the **same type** as a gold span and **overlaps** it; each gold span and each detection is matched at most once.
- **Coverage:** share of gold characters hidden by *any* detection, whatever its type. This is the leak metric: 100% means nothing of that value reached the LLM.
- **Exact:** share of correct detections whose boundaries match the gold span exactly.
- Thresholds were tuned on the **dev** split only; the numbers below are from data the tuning never saw.
